The goal is not simply to manage PPC — it is to improve the business result

As ad spend grows, small weaknesses in measurement, campaign structure or bidding can become expensive. What matters is having someone who can consistently identify wasted spend, improve conversion quality and turn more of the budget into customers.

Which PPC setup fits your business?

option 1
Manage PPC in-house

Maximum control and minimal external cost — if your team has the time and specialist PPC expertise.

Full control - You set the priorities, budgets and pace of decision-making.

A steep expertise curve - Google Ads, Performance Max and measurement evolve quickly; weak signals or structure can quietly increase CPA.

A serious time commitment - Search terms, testing, feeds, tracking and optimisation require ongoing attention, not a one-off setup.

option 2
Work with a full-service agency

Convenient when you want one partner covering several marketing disciplines.

One partner across channels - Fewer suppliers and simpler coordination across your wider marketing activity.

PPC is one of many services - Attention is shared across PPC, SEO, creative, content and other services.

Depth of PPC expertise can vary - For complex accounts, it matters who makes the decisions and how closely they work with conversion and revenue data.

option 3
Hire an in-house PPC specialist

A strong model when your business needs a specialist embedded in the team every day.

Deep business knowledge - An in-house specialist works closely with your team and can develop a detailed understanding of the product, processes and priorities.

Higher fixed cost - Salary, taxes, training and tools create a fixed cost regardless of how much specialist work is needed.

Single-person dependency - Performance depends heavily on one person's experience, capacity and ability to challenge their own assumptions.
